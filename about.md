Greetings! I'm Alec Critten (he/him), a PhD student in Computer Science at <a href="https://swansea.ac.uk">Swansea University</a> in Swansea, Wales, UK. My work is in the area of formal methods, particularly around <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satisfiability_modulo_theories">satisfiability modulo theories</a>. I'm supervised by <a href="https://csetzer.github.io/">Anton Setzer</a>, an honorary reader at Swansea now based at Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg, Sweden, and internally by <a href="https://www.swansea.ac.uk/staff/a.beckmann/">Arnold Beckmann</a> and <a href="https://www.swansea.ac.uk/staff/m.roggenbach/">Markus Roggenbach</a>. I'm affiliated with the <a href="https://www.swansea.ac.uk/compsci/research-and-impact/theoretical-computer-science/">Theoretical Computer Science research group</a> at Swansea, and I also previously served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department.
