Alec Critten (he/him) is a PhD student at <a href="https://swansea.ac.uk">Swansea University</a> (Swansea, Wales, UK) specialising in formal methods, specifically applications of satisfiability modulo theories (SMT solving). He is supervised by <a href="https://csetzer.github.io/">Anton Setzer</a> (honorary reader at Swansea, now at University of Gothenburg). He is a member of the <a href="https://www.swansea.ac.uk/compsci/research-and-impact/theoretical-computer-science/">Theoretical Computer Science research group</a> at Swansea, and also previously served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant (GTA) in the Department.
